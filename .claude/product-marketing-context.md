# Product Marketing Context — Parvalle

Read by the `copywriting` skill before any copy work. Source of truth is
[PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md); this file is the marketing-facing summary.

## What we sell

**Parvalle Course Manager** — digital infrastructure for independent course
providers. It turns an existing WordPress site into a complete booking and
back-office system for courses, seminars and workshops.

Parvalle is **not**: generic booking software, appointment software, a CRM, an
LMS, a generic WordPress plugin, or "all-in-one business software". Do not
reposition it as any of these.

## Audience

Independent and small professional course providers in DACH: solo providers,
workshop providers, studios, small schools/academies, multi-course providers
with several instructors or locations. They sell **scheduled** courses,
workshops, seminars, trainings and multi-session programmes.

Think in operating reality, not demographics. No lifestyle personas.

## Awareness level: PROBLEM-AWARE

They know something is frustrating: administration, fragmented tools, booking
forms, spreadsheets, manual participant lists, chasing payments, PayPal links,
reconciling bank transfers, waitlists, cancellations, invoices, repeated emails,
course dates, participant communication, several systems at once.

They are **not** searching for "WordPress Kursmanagement Software" or for
"Parvalle". Meet them at the problem they already recognise.

## Framing rule (non-negotiable)

Course providers are **not bad at running their business**. Their business grew
or became operationally complex enough that manual work and disconnected tools
create friction. Respect the work they already do. No patronising language. Do
not dramatise ordinary admin into a crisis.

## Value architecture

**Verkaufen. Betreiben. Verstehen.** Purpose direction: *Mehr Leidenschaft.
Weniger Management.* — a strategic direction, not a tagline to paste into every
section.

## The differentiator

A course is not an appointment. A six-week course can be one offer, one
booking, one payment and one participant group while still containing six
individual dates. A booking for three places can represent the three actual
people attending. Explain this in customer language, never database language.

The operating chain: Interessent → Kurs → Buchung → Platz → Zahlung →
Teilnehmer → Kommunikation → Durchführung → Änderung/Storno → Buchhaltung →
Analyse → nächste Buchung.

## Benefit priority (communication order)

1. Online booking on the provider's own website
2. Payments and deposits
3. Automated communication
4. Invoicing / accounting / DACH depth
5. Capacity and waitlist
6. Cancellations and refunds
7. Course series and group bookings
8. Participant management and self-service
9. Catalogue / discoverability
10. Reviews · 11. Coupons/extras · 12. Abandoned-booking recovery · 13. Partner system

Not every capability gets equal prominence. Long feature lists live low in the
hierarchy.

## Proof we may use

Product truth only: own WordPress site · no Parvalle marketplace · own customer
relationship · reliable capacity control (no overbooking, even on simultaneous
access) · server-side controlled payments · DACH invoicing (§33, §14, GoBD,
DATEV, Steuerberater-CSV, Verfahrensdokumentation) · DSGVO mechanisms · data
control · already-created items keep working when a Pro licence lapses.

## Proof we may NOT invent

Testimonials · customer numbers · customer logos · reviews · awards ·
market-share · conversion statistics · time-saving percentages · revenue
improvements · guarantees · integrations · functionality · certifications ·
pricing · scarcity. No fake examples that read as customer evidence.
Hypothetical examples only when obviously framed as examples.

**No prices are approved for publication. No signup, trial, demo or sales-call
flow exists** — do not write CTAs that imply one.

## Editions

- **Free** — a real operational foundation, not a demo. The provider can genuinely publish and run courses.
- **Pro** — more automation, revenue protection, workflow depth, compliance depth, advanced analysis.

Pro is not "the good version". Free must stay credible. The upgrade moment
arises when the business creates the job: course full → waitlist; high-value
course → deposit; payment overdue → reminder; multi-session programme → course
series; month-end → DATEV.

Licence principle: when Pro lapses, everything already created keeps working;
only starting new Pro actions pauses.

## Dashboard / Verstehen philosophy

The point is not "more analytics" — it is understanding the business without
being misled by vanity metrics: what needs attention today, how bookings,
occupancy and revenue develop, how customers behave, with reliable definitions
and honest limits. Never promise conversion rates without a real denominator,
LTV without identity depth, predictions without history, or attendance without
attendance data. This honesty is part of the brand.

## Voice

Intelligent, calm, specific, grown-up, confident, human, direct, grounded,
professional, understated. Natural German — not translated English SaaS.

Never: loud, American-SaaS, hype-driven, exaggerated, gratuitously playful,
corporate, bureaucratic, AI-generated, over-polished. Avoid excessive
rhetorical questions. No exclamation marks. Vary sentence structure; no
"Mit Parvalle kannst du X. So sparst du Y. Dadurch hast du Z." patterns.

### Words that must earn their place

einfach · effizient · nahtlos · optimal · flexibel · leistungsstark ·
innovativ · intelligent · professionell · modern · alles an einem Ort ·
Zeit sparen

If describing what actually happens makes the sentence stronger, replace the
abstraction.

## Deviations from the generic copywriting skill

The skill recommends social proof bars, stats sections, testimonials,
guarantees and risk reversal. **Parvalle has none of these approved.** Substitute
product proof, workflow proof, technical credibility and transparent policy.
The skill also encourages rhetorical questions and humour; use both sparingly
here — the brand is understated.
